This privacy policy explains what data the Flashlight app (“the app”) handles and how.

Summary

  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The camera permission is used only to switch the flashlight (torch) on and off. The app never takes photos or videos.

Permissions

Camera / flashlight. On Android, the flashlight is part of the camera hardware, so the app asks for camera permission to control it. The app uses this permission only to turn the torch on and off (including the SOS blink). It does not capture, record, store, or send any images or video.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, storage, or other unrelated permissions.

Data storage

The app does not store personal data on your device or on any server. App settings such as the light or dark theme stay on your device only.

Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not send any data to us or to any third party.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no data from any user, including children.

Data deletion

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing to delete. You can uninstall the app at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. If a future version of the app introduces advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
