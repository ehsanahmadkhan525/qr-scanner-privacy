This privacy policy explains what data the QR & Barcode Scanner app (“the app”) handles and how.

Summary

  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Your scan and generation history is stored only on your device.
  • The camera is used solely to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded or stored.

Information the app handles

Camera. When you use the scanner, the app accesses your camera to detect codes. Processing happens entirely on-device in real time. The app does not save camera frames and does not transmit them anywhere. If you choose to scan a code from an image in your gallery, that image is read on-device only to detect a code.

Scan & generation history. Codes you scan or generate are saved locally on your device (in the app’s private storage) so you can view them in the History tab. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete an entry, clear history, or uninstall the app.

Saved images. When you save a generated QR code, the image is written to your device’s gallery using the system media APIs. The app does not upload it.

Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not send your data to any third party.

Barcode detection uses Google’s on-device ML Kit barcode scanning, which runs locally on your device through Google Play services. Scanned content is not sent to Google by the app.

If you tap a link, email address, or phone number from a scan result, the app hands it to another app you have chosen (such as your browser or dialer). What happens next is governed by that app’s own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera — required to scan codes.
  • Internet — the app does not send your data anywhere; this permission is retained for standard platform and Play services functionality.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — used to save generated QR images to your gallery.

The app requests permission only when needed and does not request location, contacts, microphone, or other unrelated permissions.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no data from any user, including children.

Data deletion

All app data is stored locally. To delete it, clear history within the app or uninstall the app.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. If a future version of the app introduces advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
